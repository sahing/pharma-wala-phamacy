Practical Health Tips for Families

Chronic Care · 2 min read

Taking Blood Pressure & Thyroid Medicines on Schedule

Thyroid tablets like levothyroxine absorb best on an empty stomach 45–60 minutes before morning tea, while BP tablets work most reliably when taken at the same hour daily.

Monsoon & Summer Safety · 2 min read

Proper Medicine Storage in Humid Kolkata Weather

Keep tablet strips inside their original foil blisters away from kitchen heat and bathroom steam. Always inspect moisture-sensitive capsules and close diabetes test-strip vials immediately.

Diabetes Monitoring · 2 min read

Getting Accurate Readings from Home Glucometers

Wash hands with soap and dry thoroughly before testing. Avoid squeezing the fingertip hard, and record fasting and 2-hour post-meal readings to share at your polyclinic follow-up.
