Return & Refund Policy

1. Eligible Returns

Unopened, undamaged, full medicine strips and sealed healthcare items with the original printed Pharmawala Pharmacy bill may be returned or exchanged within 7 days of purchase or delivery.

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Temperature-sensitive cold-chain products (insulin vials, vaccines, eye drops requiring refrigeration).
  • Partially cut tablet strips, opened bottles, broken manufacturer seals, or hygiene items (thermometers, nebulizer masks, diapers once opened).

3. Wrong or Damaged Delivery

If an incorrect or damaged item is delivered, notify us within 24 hours on WhatsApp or call 8282082425 for a free replacement or full refund.
