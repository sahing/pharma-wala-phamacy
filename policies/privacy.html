Privacy Policy

How Pharmawala Pharmacy handles your prescription images, phone number, and delivery information.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order request or upload a prescription through Pharmawala Pharmacy, we only use the details you voluntarily share—such as patient name, mobile number, Baruipur delivery address, and prescription images sent via WhatsApp or email.

2. Prescription & Medical Data Confidentiality

Prescription files previewed on this website remain locally inside your browser until you send them via WhatsApp (8282082425) or email (pharmawalapharmacy@gmail.com). Our registered pharmacist reviews prescriptions solely for verifying Schedule H/H1 compliance and fulfilling your medicine order. We never sell or share medical records with advertisers or third parties.

3. Browser Local Storage

We store your shopping cart items in your device's browser localStorage strictly so your selected medicines remain available while browsing. You may clear your cart or browser storage at any time.

4. Contact for Privacy Requests

For questions or requests to delete your order records, email pharmawalapharmacy@gmail.com or call 8282082425.
