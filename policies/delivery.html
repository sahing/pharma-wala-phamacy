Local Delivery Information

1. Serviceable Areas

We provide home delivery across Uttar Khodarbazar, Baruipur-Kalyanpur Road, Baruipur Station area, Subuddhipur, Padmapukur, and surrounding neighbourhoods in PIN 700144.

2. Delivery Charges

  • Orders ₹499 and above: FREE local doorstep delivery.

3. Delivery Timings & Payment

Orders confirmed on WhatsApp or phone before 7:30 PM are typically delivered the same day. Orders received after 7:30 PM are delivered the following morning. Pay securely via Cash on Delivery or UPI upon inspecting your sealed order and bill.
